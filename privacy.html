Privacy Policy — Abyss Hunters

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Abyss Hunters ("the game") is developed by Abdulrahman ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and what choices you have. Contact: Abdulrahman.hussienn@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect ourselves

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location.

The only things the game stores are kept on your own device and never sent to us:

  • the diver name you type, your chosen species and skin, and your settings (sound, frame rate);
  • the address of the last multiplayer server you joined.

You can erase all of it by deleting the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android) and other device identifiers;
  • technical information about your device, operating system and app version;
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address;
  • how you interact with the ads shown.

Google uses this to deliver and measure ads and, if you agree, to personalise them. Google acts as an independent controller for this data. Their policies describe it in full:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services:

    • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive any of this data in a form that identifies you. We only see aggregate counts of impressions and revenue in the AdMob dashboard.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows a consent

    • message the first time you play. You choose whether to allow personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Ad settings in the game.

  • On iOS, the game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before

    • any tracking takes place. If you refuse, the game works exactly the same and you see non-personalised ads instead.

  • On any device you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in the system settings

    • (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

3. Multiplayer

When you join a multiplayer server, your device connects directly to that server. The server receives your IP address (unavoidable for any internet connection), the diver name you chose, and your position in the game world, so other players can see you. Game servers keep short-lived operational logs (connections and errors) for stability and abuse prevention, and these are deleted within 30 days. Nothing from multiplayer is used for advertising.

4. Children

Abyss Hunters is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, write to Abdulrahman.hussienn@gmail.com and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and your rights

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we hold nothing to export or delete. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device. For data held by Google as an advertising provider, use the links in section 2 or contact Google directly.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, and to object to processing. Write to Abdulrahman.hussienn@gmail.com and we will help, including by pointing you to the right contact at Google where the data is theirs.

7. Security

The game stores nothing sensitive. Multiplayer traffic carries only gameplay information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with a new date, and a notice is shown in the game when the change is significant.
