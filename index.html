Frequently asked

How do I play? Drag the left stick to swim, drag anywhere else to look, and tap ATTACK to bite. Look up and swim forward to climb towards the surface. The animals of the ice walk with the same stick and use JUMP to climb back out of the water.

How do I grow? Eat. Every fish counts towards the next of five growth stages, and each stage unlocks a mutation slot.

Why did I die so fast? An empty stomach gives you about a minute, air breathers drown under water, fish suffocate out of it, and land animals need to drink.

Does it cost anything? No. The game is free and shows ads. There are no purchases and nothing is pay-to-win.

Can I turn off personalised ads? Yes — Settings → Ad settings, where the option applies, or in your device's privacy settings.

Privacy

The game has no accounts and collects nothing about you itself. Ads are served by Google AdMob. The full policy is here: Privacy Policy.
